# Active slice register

Last reviewed: 23 November 2026. This is the current, ordered register; the [June 2026 roadmap](production-readiness-roadmap.md) is a historical baseline, not a count of remaining work. The [implementation log](implementation-slices.md) records completed slices. One implementation slice is completed, tested, and committed on `main` before the next is started, unless the owner explicitly changes that cadence.

## Current baseline and ordering principle

- The app already has Area Office, HQ, audit, finance and archival claim workflows, an archived-packet PDF, an 8th-edition calculator with approved activation records, in-app compliance foundations, a Preview-only eligibility test provider, and a Remita **contribution collection** integration. None of those establishes live reimbursement disbursement or a real Payportal eligibility contract.
- Archived-claim browsing exists for authorized HQ and jurisdictional Area Office staff, but it is not yet a litigation-ready record. Its long-term evidence access, completeness, historical jurisdiction, and award explanation need verification.
- Prioritize trustworthy financial facts, explanation, and archive before analytics. Introduce an eligibility-mode contract before turning on any new compliance source. Integrate Workspace identity before live disbursement so payment authority and audit identity are unambiguous. Run production-hardening checks throughout, not only at the end.
- `Ready` means the slice can be scoped from current code and approved rules; `Policy/contract` means implementation may begin only after the named decision or external contract is available. The ordering is recommended, not permission to skip a dependency.

## Open decisions to close before implementation reaches them

1. **Awards and archive:** Which changes require a mandatory written explanation; who signs the final award narrative; whether archived notes permit attributed addenda; retention and legal-hold periods.
2. **Analytics:** Exact definitions of “paid”, “awarded”, “outstanding”, “claim year”, and “Area Office”; whether figures follow the office at submission or current jurisdiction; HQ RO geographic scope and export limits.
3. **Compliance:** Whether manual evidence is sufficient only after an ITF officer verifies it; Payportal API contract and outage policy; approval to operate in-app compliance; treatment of a mode change while a claim is in flight.
4. **Identity:** Workspace stable ID, app-entitlement source, local-account matching, global sign-out and emergency login procedure.
5. **Disbursement:** Government authority and funding-account ownership, dual-control/signature chain, Remita funds-transfer contract, whether payouts may be batched, how reversals and unknown outcomes are reconciled, and who may change the active payout mode.

## Maintaining the register

At the completion of **every** implementation slice, in the same commit:

1. Update its state here to `Completed` with commit and test evidence; split or add follow-on slices if discoveries changed the scope. Keep the remaining order and external blockers accurate.
2. Append a dated entry to the [implementation log](implementation-slices.md), covering behavior, migration/environment changes, security/authorization, and how to assess it locally and in staging.
3. Update affected user/developer documentation and automated tests. Confirm `git diff --check`, relevant tests/build, and a clean commit on `main` before asking for the next go-ahead. Do not include `.env` or secrets.

This register update itself is a planning/documentation slice; it does not mark any of the implementations above complete.
