# Active slice register

Last reviewed: 25 November 2026. This is the current, ordered register; the [June 2026 roadmap](production-readiness-roadmap.md) is a historical baseline, not a count of remaining work. The [implementation log](implementation-slices.md) records completed slices. One implementation slice is completed, tested, and committed on `main` before the next is started, unless the owner explicitly changes that cadence.

## Current baseline and ordering principle

- The app already has Area Office, HQ, audit, finance and archival claim workflows, an archived-packet PDF, an 8th-edition calculator with approved activation records, in-app compliance foundations, a Preview-only eligibility test provider, and a Remita **contribution collection** integration. None of those establishes live reimbursement disbursement or a real Payportal eligibility contract.
- Version 2 archived packets now freeze claim, calculation, review, payment, award explanation, and evidence-index facts at settlement. Claim-scoped evidence links and original-office attribution are in place. Storage-level write-once retention, legal hold and the eventual electronic document-management-server contract remain operational/policy follow-ons.
- Prioritize trustworthy financial facts, explanation, and archive before analytics. Introduce an eligibility-mode contract before turning on any new compliance source. Integrate Workspace identity before live disbursement so payment authority and audit identity are unambiguous. Run production-hardening checks throughout, not only at the end.
- `Implemented` means the defined slice was delivered and verified; `Pending` means the slice is not complete, even if supporting foundations exist; `Ongoing` is a cross-cutting gate that remains open. `Ready` means the pending slice can be scoped from current code and approved rules; `Policy/contract` means implementation may begin only after the named decision or external contract is available. The ordering is recommended, not permission to skip a dependency.

## Open decisions to close before implementation reaches them

1. **Awards and archive:** Which changes require a mandatory written explanation, and who formally signs the final award narrative. Staff-only append-only addenda are now available. Retain records indefinitely until ITF approves a formal policy; define legal hold, backup, immutable object storage, and transfer to the future electronic document-management server before claiming full records-policy compliance.
2. **Analytics:** The [service contract](claim-analytics-contract.md) defines provisional figures and original-office attribution; the HQ RO's assigned-plus-archived scope is approved. Operational exports now use the same three FSD roles, 5,000-row cap, no personal bank/staff fields, report ID and generation timestamp. Finance must sign off the dictionary, official export authority/retention and whether signed point-in-time snapshots are required. Decide whether the older management dashboard should adopt historical-office attribution.
3. **Compliance:** Whether manual evidence is sufficient only after an ITF officer verifies it; Payportal API contract and outage policy; approval to operate in-app compliance; treatment of a mode change while a claim is in flight.
4. **Identity:** Workspace stable ID, app-entitlement source, local-account matching, global sign-out and emergency login procedure.
5. **Disbursement:** Government authority and funding-account ownership, dual-control/signature chain, Remita funds-transfer contract, whether payouts may be batched, how reversals and unknown outcomes are reconciled, and who may change the active payout mode.

## Maintaining the register

At the completion of **every** implementation slice, in the same commit:

1. Update its implementation status here to `Implemented` with commit and test evidence; split or add follow-on slices if discoveries changed the scope. Keep the remaining order and external blockers accurate.
2. Append a dated entry to the [implementation log](implementation-slices.md), covering behavior, migration/environment changes, security/authorization, and how to assess it locally and in staging.
3. Update affected user/developer documentation and automated tests. Confirm `git diff --check`, relevant tests/build, and a clean commit on `main` before asking for the next go-ahead. Do not include `.env` or secrets.

This register update itself is a planning/documentation slice; it does not mark any of the implementations above complete.
